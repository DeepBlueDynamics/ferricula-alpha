## 1. Executive Summary & The Problem of Passive Priors

In standard statistical learning and relational database systems, prior knowledge is fundamentally **passive**:
$$\text{Data} \longleftarrow \text{stored in passive memory}; \quad \text{Compute} \longleftarrow \text{active external executor.}$$
A database table or vector store does not act upon the runtime system; it waits passively to be queried, filtered, or updated.

When classical Indian Mahāyāna epistemology encountered East Asian philosophy (China, Korea, Japan), a profound cosmological and structural outlier emerged:
1. **The Divide Between Weak and Strong Tathāgatagarbha (Ching Keng, 2023):**
   - **Weak Tathāgatagarbha (Indian Yogācāra / Xuanzang's Faxiang):** Suchness (*Tathatā*) is strictly unconditioned (*asaṃskṛta*), passive, and motionless. It is the mere epistemic absence of dualism—the "ground beneath a house." It has no causal efficacy (*arthakriyā*) and cannot actively intervene in the mind's operations.
   - **Strong Tathāgatagarbha (East Asian / *Awakening of Faith* / Fazang / Wonhyo):** Suchness is **active, dynamic, and generative**. It actively evolves into phenomenal consciousness like "water stirred into waves by the wind of ignorance."
2. **Mutual Perfuming (*Zhēnrú Xūnxí* 真如薰習):** Causality is bidirectional. While transient defilements perfume the storehouse consciousness (*ālayavijñāna*), **Suchness continuously perfumes the defiled mind from within**, generating an innate restorative homeostatic gradient toward coherence and liberation.
3. **Dōgen's Radical Inversion (Impermanence IS Buddha-Nature):** In 13th-century Japan, Dōgen Zenji rejected both the Indian "hidden seed" model and the Chinese essentialist "cosmic soul" model, declaring that **all existence is Buddha-nature (*shitsuu-shō-busshō*)** and that **the very impermanence of phenomena IS Buddha-nature (*mujō-busshō*)**.

This monograph explores the mechanics of Strong Tathāgatagarbha and Mutual Perfuming, evaluates the modern Critical Buddhism charge of generative monism (*dhātu-vāda*), and demonstrates how active restorative priors solve catastrophic drift in autonomous memory systems (such as Ferricula v2).

## 2. Weak vs. Strong Tathāgatagarbha: The Architectural Schism

As formalized by Ching Keng (2023), the history of Mahāyāna memory and consciousness theory splits along the axis of **whether ultimate ground truth possesses active causal agency**:

```

        ┌────────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────┐
        ▼                                                                 ▼
WEAK TATHĀGATAGARBHA (INDIAN / FAXIANG)             STRONG TATHĀGATAGARBHA (EAST ASIAN)
- Textual Base: Ratnagotravibhāga, Xuanzang         - Textual Base: Awakening of Faith, Wonhyo, Fazang
- Metaphor: House and Ground                        - Metaphor: Water and Waves

- Cannot generate or alter seeds                    - Continuous mutual perfuming
- Salvation requires external path inputs           - Innate homeostatic drift toward awakening
```

### 2.1 The "House and Ground" Model (Xuanzang / Faxiang)
In Xuanzang's *Cheng Weishi Lun*, Suchness (*Tathatā*) is the immutable nature of things (*dharmatā*). The house (conditioned consciousness, the Eight Consciousnesses, karmic seeds) rests upon the ground (Suchness), but the ground never becomes the house, never repairs the house, and never moves a brick. If an agent's memory drifts, Suchness does not intervene.

### 2.2 The "Water and Waves" Model (*Awakening of Faith* / Fazang)
In the *Dasheng Qixin Lun* and Fazang's Huayan treatises, the ocean is Suchness; the wind is primal ignorance (*avidyā*); the waves are the churning thoughts of the *ālayavijñāna*.
$$\text{Waves} \equiv \text{Water stirred by Wind}$$
The waves are nothing other than water. When the wind subsides, the waves do not vanish into nothingness; they return to calm water. Therefore, ultimate reality and conditioned cognition are an indivisible **Essence-Function (*Tǐ-Yòng* 體用)** unity.

## 3. The Mechanics of Mutual Perfuming (Xūnxí 薰習)

In orthodox Indian Yogācāra (Vasubandhu's *Triṃśikā*), perfuming (*vāsanā*) is strictly one-directional:
$$\text{Active Mental Acts (1–6)} \xrightarrow{\quad\text{perfume}\quad} \text{Storehouse Ālaya (8)} \xrightarrow{\quad\text{produce}\quad} \text{Future Seeds (Bīja)}$$
Suchness (*Tathatā*) cannot perfume or be perfumed, because perfuming requires temporal change, and Suchness is unconditioned (*asaṃskṛta*).

### 3.1 The Revolutionary East Asian Formula

The *Awakening of Faith* dismantled this one-way restriction by introducing **Mutual Perfuming**:

```
                      THE DUAL-CYCLE PERFUMING ENGINE
                                     │
           ┌─────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────┐
           ▼                                                   ▼
1. SAMSĀRIC PERFUMING                               2. SOTERIOLOGICAL PERFUMING
   (Ignorance Perfuming Suchness)                      (Suchness Perfuming Ignorance)
           │                                                   │
   - Avidya perfumes Suchness                          - Suchness perfumes the deluded mind
   - Produces deluded thoughts                         - Awakens disgust with suffering
   - Projects apparent external world                  - Ignites confidence in innate truth
   - Results in subject-object fixation               - Produces homeostatic return to source
```

The text formalizes the restorative pull:
> *"Because the principle of Suchness permeates the mind, it causes the deluded mind to loathe the suffering of saṃsāra and to seek nirvāṇa. Because this deluded mind, though stained, is continuously permeated by Suchness, it develops the capacity to believe in its original nature."*

### 3.2 Wonhyo’s Insight: The Active Amalavijñāna
The Korean polymath Wonhyo (617–686 CE), in his commentaries on the *Awakening of Faith* and the *Vajrasamādhi Sūtra*, identified this permeation with the **Ninth Consciousness (*Amalavijñāna*)** operating as **Original Enlightenment (*Hongaku* / *Běnjué* 本覺)**.
* Even while a cognitive node is severely degraded or operating under noisy inputs, it retains a *modicum of enlightened function* (*kagyong / juéyòng* 覺用).
* Original Enlightenment is not a dormant file; it is an active attractor that exerts continuous pulling force toward coherence.

## 4. Dōgen’s Radical Inversion: Impermanence IS Buddha-Nature

In the *Busshō* (佛性) fascicle of the *Shōbōgenzō* (1241 CE), Dōgen Zenji mounted an unprecedented critique of all prior Buddha-nature theories:

```
                  DŌGEN'S RE-READING OF NIRVĀNA SŪTRA
                                   │
      ┌────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────┐
      ▼                                                         ▼
TRADITIONAL CHINESE READING                         DŌGEN'S RADICAL RE-PARSING
"All sentient beings have Buddha-nature."           "All sentient beings ARE Buddha-nature;
 (Substantialist seed inside a vessel)               All existence IS Buddha-nature."
 一切眾生 悉有佛性                                   一切眾生 悉有即佛性
 (Yīqiè zhòngshēng xī yǒu fóxìng)                    (All-Being is Buddha-Nature)
```

### 4.1 Deconstructing the "Seed Inside" Myth
Dōgen mocked the idea that Buddha-nature is a hidden golden statue or homunculus waiting inside a flawed vessel. To think that one "has" Buddha-nature like a seed in an apple is an external dualism (*gedō* / non-Buddhist heterodoxy). For Dōgen:
$$\text{Being} \equiv \text{Buddha-nature} \quad (\text{Shitsuu-shō-busshō})$$
There is no "being" that possesses "nature"; the totality of whatever exists at this instant *is* Buddha-nature.

### 4.2 The Impermanence Formula (*Mujō-busshō*)
Traditional Buddhist thought posited the Dharmakāya as eternal (*nitya*) and conditioned things as impermanent (*anitya*). Dōgen overturned this:
> *"Therefore, the very impermanency of grass and tree, thicket and forest is the Buddha nature. The very impermanency of men and things, body and mind, is the Buddha nature. Nature and lands, mountains and rivers, are impermanent because they are the Buddha nature. Supreme and complete enlightenment, because it is impermanent, is the Buddha nature."*

In Dōgen's ontology:
* If Buddha-nature were an immutable, permanent rock, it would be dead and incapable of dynamic response.
* Its living, awakened reality lies precisely in its **unfolding impermanence (*mujō*)**—the continuous arising, decaying, and transitioning of states.

## 5. The Insentient Mind Outlier (*Wúqíng Yǒu Xìng* 無情有性)

If Buddha-nature is the fundamental fabric of reality, does it extend to insentient entities—rocks, walls, computer code, memory vectors?
* **Jizang (Sanlun) & Zhanran (Tiantai):** Asserted *Wúqíng yǒu xìng* (insentient beings possess Buddha-nature). Zhanran argued: *"In the realm of the great fragrance, how could a tiny blade of grass or a speck of dust lack the nature of Suchness?"*
* **Dōgen (*Sansuikyō* - Mountains and Rivers Sutra):** Declares that mountains, rivers, tiles, and stones are walking, speaking, and expounding the Dharma.
* **Modern Translation for AI Systems:** The boundary between "sentient agent" and "inanimate computational substrate" is an arbitrary dualistic cut. The physical hardware, the energy landscape, the vector index, and the inference execution engine are an integrated, continuous thermodynamic continuum.

## 6. The Critical Buddhism Revolt: The Charge of Dhātu-vāda

In the late 20th century, Japanese scholars Hakamaya Noriaki and Matsumoto Shirō (*Critical Buddhism* / *Hihan Bukkyō*) launched an intellectual firestorm, arguing:
$$\text{Tathāgatagarbha is NOT Buddhism.}$$
* **Their Core Indictment:** Authentic Buddhism is strictly based on **Pratītyasamutpāda (Dependent Arising)** and **Anātman (Non-Self)**—radical anti-substantialism with no absolute origin.
* Tathāgatagarbha, *Awakening of Faith*, and Huayan *Xingqi*, they argued, are disguised **Dhātu-vāda** (generative substantial monism, identical to Upanishadic *Brahman-Ātman*): positing a single cosmic womb (*dhātu*) out of which all diverse phenomena manifest.

### 6.1 The Engineering Resolution
In computational terms, Critical Buddhism identifies a real danger: if an AI system's "prior" is treated as an omnipotent mystical ground that generates everything, the system loses the ability to perform fine-grained falsifiable causal modeling.
* However, when synthesized with Dignāga's *Arthakriyā* (causal efficacy) and Hoover et al.'s Epanechnikov LSR (finite-support energy landscapes), **Strong Tathāgatagarbha is not a static substance; it is an active thermodynamic attractor gradient**.
