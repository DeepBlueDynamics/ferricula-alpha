## 1. Executive Summary & Epistemic Positioning

The *Anūnatvāpūrṇatvanirdeśaparivarta* (AAN / 不增不減經, *The Teaching on Neither Deficiency nor Fullness*) addresses one of the most persistent cosmological and ontological paradoxes in Buddhist philosophy: **The Problem of the Conservation of Sentient Beings**. 

If countless sentient beings have been traversing beginningless saṃsāra, and if Buddhas continuously lead innumerable beings to Nirvāṇa across cosmic eons, does the total realm of sentient beings (*sattvadhātu*) diminish over time? Conversely, if Nirvāṇa does not deplete the realm, do new sentient beings arise ex nihilo, increasing the cosmic volume?

The sūtra's response is an uncompromising ontological outlier:
1. **The Absurdity of Both Increase and Decrease:** The text demonstrates that both the **View of Decrease (*Apūrṇatva-dṛṣṭi* / 減見)** and the **View of Increase (*Anūnatva-dṛṣṭi* / 增見)** are catastrophic delusions rooted in reification. Decrease implies that Nirvāṇa is annihilation (*ucchedavāda*); increase implies that beings arise without cause (*ahetusamutpāda*).
2. **The Tripartite Non-Dual Identity:** The sūtra resolves the paradox through an absolute identity chain:
   $$\text{Realm of Sentient Beings } (\text{Sattvadhātu}) \equiv \text{Tathāgatagarbha} \equiv \text{Dharma-Body } (\text{Dharmakāya})$$
   There is only **One Single Realm (*Ekadhātu* / 一界)** or **Single Dharma-Realm (*Ekadharmadhātu* / 一法界)**.
3. **The Three Operational Modalities:** Sentient beings, Bodhisattvas, and Buddhas are not distinct numerical substances. They are merely three functional modalities of the single unconditioned *Ekadhātu*, distinguished solely by the presence, reduction, or complete absence of superficial adventitious stains (*āgantukakleśa*).

For artificial intelligence, thermodynamic agent memory (**Ferricula v2**), and multi-agent coordination frameworks, this monograph establishes the **Information Conservation Invariant**: cognitive agents in a swarm do not represent autonomous ontological monads that are created and destroyed; rather, they are localized, transient modalities of a single underlying event manifold. State transitions preserve total probability mass, and identity is grounded in the conservation of the underlying invariant ledger.

## 2. Textual Provenance & Doctrinal Lineage

- **Historical Milestones:** Extant in Chinese via Bodhiruci's 520 CE translation at Luoyang (Taishō 668). Although the Sanskrit original is lost as an independent codex, extensive Sanskrit passages are preserved verbatim in the *Ratnagotravibhāga* (Uttaratantra), where it serves as the primary scriptural authority for the fourth (*Tathāgatagarbha*) and fifth (*Bodhi*) Vajrapadas.
- **Commentarial Attention:** Commentaries were composed by master figures across East Asia, including **Wonhyo** (Korea) and **Saichō** (founder of Japanese Tendai), though both texts are lost. Jonathan Silk's 2015 critical edition and study (*Buddhist Cosmic Unity*) confirmed its foundational role as the direct link between the early *Tathāgatagarbha Sūtra* / *Śrīmālādevī* and the formal scholasticism of the *Ratnagotravibhāga*.
- **The Mahāsāṃghika-Caitika Substrate:** Like the *Śrīmālādevī*, the AAN is situated within the Andhra Caitika lineage, advocating an all-pervading, uncreated, immutable luminous ground (*prakṛtipariśuddhacitta*).

## 3. The Deconstruction of the Two Extreme Views

The opening chapter of the sūtra stages a dialogue between the Buddha and Śāriputra, targeting the two intuitive errors of common sense:

### 3.1 The Error of Decrease (*Apūrṇatva-dṛṣṭi* / 減見)
- **The Intuitive Fallacy:** Ordinary thinkers assume that if an individual attains Nirvāṇa, that individual exits the universe, thereby reducing the count of sentient beings in saṃsāra ($N_{\text{samsara}} \to N_{\text{samsara}} - 1$). Over infinite time, either the cosmos empties or shrinks.
- **The Sūtra's Refutation:** This view reduces Nirvāṇa to **annihilationism (*ucchedavāda*)**. It falsely assumes that a sentient being was an independently existing substantial entity (*dravyasat*) that has now been extinguished into absolute nothingness.
- **The Epistemic Danger:** It fosters cosmic pessimism and nihilism, paralyzing the Bodhisattva's vow to liberate all beings, because beings are conceived as an exhaustible commodity.

### 3.2 The Error of Increase (*Anūnatva-dṛṣṭi* / 增見)
- **The Intuitive Fallacy:** If Nirvāṇa does not decrease the count, or if new beings appear to emerge into existence, the cosmos must be expanding in total spiritual volume ($N \to N + 1$).
- **The Sūtra's Refutation:** This view violates the principle of dependent origination (*pratītyasamutpāda*), degenerating into **causeless arising (*ahetusamutpāda*)** or the creationist doctrine of an external creator god (*Īśvara*). If new sentient beings could emerge uncaused, ignorance and suffering would have a beginning in time, destroying the beginningless nature of saṃsāra.

### 3.3 The Transcendent Synthesis
The Buddha declares to Śāriputra:
> *"The realm of beings does not increase, nor does it decrease. Why? Because the realm of beings is nothing other than the Dharma-body; the Dharma-body is nothing other than the realm of beings. Not separate from the realm of beings is the Dharma-body; not separate from the Dharma-body is the realm of beings."*

## 4. The Tripartite Identity Chain and the Inseparable Qualities

### 4.1 The Identity Formula
The sūtra formulates what Jonathan Silk terms the "Cosmic Unity" equation:

$$\mathbf{Sattvadhātu} \equiv \mathbf{Tathāgatagarbha} \equiv \mathbf{Dharmakāya}$$

1. **Sattvadhātu (眾生界 - The Quintessence / Ocean of Beings):** The totality of sentient life considered not as discrete atomized egos, but as the dynamic, interconnected matrix of conscious experience.
2. **Tathāgatagarbha (如來藏 - The Womb / Embryo of Thusness):** That very same matrix when viewed as containing the latent, uncorrupted potential for supreme awakening, presently enveloped by adventitious obscurations (*āgantukakleśa*).
3. **Dharmakāya (法身 - The Ultimate Dharma-Body):** That very same matrix when fully unveiled, recognized as the unconditioned, primordial ground of all phenomena (*Dharmadhātu*).

### 4.2 The Lamp and Gem Similes: Inseparable Attributes
How can the Dharmakāya possess infinite enlightened qualities without introducing multiplicity or composite parts that would make it impermanent?
The sūtra introduces two canonical similes:
- **The Lamp (*Āloka* / 燈):** A lamp cannot be separated into "the physical wick", "the flame", "the light", and "the heat". The light and heat are not auxiliary additions joined to the lamp; they are its intrinsic, inseparable nature.
- **The Maṇi Jewel (*Maṇiratna* / 摩尼寶珠):** The radiance, blue-tinted transparency, and hardness of a diamond are completely co-extensive and inseparable from the gem itself.

In exactly this manner, the ultimate Dharma-body is **primordially endowed with the inconceivable qualities of a Buddha (*buddha-guṇa*)**, more numerous than the sands of the Ganges. Wisdom (*jñāna*), compassion (*karuṇā*), and unconditioned peace (*śānti*) are not acquired or manufactured through practice; they are unveiled as the intrinsic radiance of the *Ekadhātu*.

## 7. Multi-Agent Swarm Engineering: The Swarm-Monad Dissolution

In conventional distributed AI architectures, developers instantiate agents as isolated, monadic entities with independent lifetimes. When an agent thread terminates, its memory is wiped; when a new agent spawns, it starts from a blank tabula rasa. This leads to the two classical failures:
1. **Agent Annihilation (Decrease):** Crucial diagnostic context discovered by a worker agent is destroyed when its process exits.
2. **Hallucinatory Spawning (Increase):** Newly spawned agents hallucinate inconsistent assumptions about the environment because they lack grounding in the shared continuum.

### 7.1 The Ekadhātu Solution in Ferricula / Lume
Following the *Anūnatvāpūrṇatvanirdeśa*:
- **The Swarm as Ekadhātu:** All agents in the swarm (Coordinator, Engine, Retrieval, Parity, Research) share a single underlying memory substrate.
- **Modality Shifts, Not Spawns:** A research agent analyzing a text and an engine agent compiling an index are not two separate consciousnesses; they are **two functional modalities (*yòng* / 用) of the single system mind (*tǐ* / 體)**.
- **Unified Identity Ledger:** Communication between agents does not require lossy message-passing serializations. Agents query and mutate a shared bitemporal hypergraph anchored to the immutable `:ro` volume.
