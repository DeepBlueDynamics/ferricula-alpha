## 1. Executive Summary & Research Scope

Following the human's direction to expand the research outlook and inspect the live **Eight Consciousnesses** framework (a Hyperia web pane), this research briefing investigates the critical **doctrinal, historical, and epistemic outliers** that surround the standard Yogācāra eight-consciousness taxonomy (*aṣṭa-vijñānakāyāḥ*).

Standard pedagogical summaries often present the eight consciousnesses as a static, compartmentalized stack: five sensory faculties (1–5), mental scratchpad (6, *manovijñāna*), ego/utility filter (7, *kliṣṭamanas*), and storehouse repository (8, *ālayavijñāna*). However, deep scholastic analysis reveals major theological controversies, divergent sectarian lineages, and radical epistemological critiques.

These outliers are not mere sectarian curiosities. For autonomous cognitive agent memory (**Ferricula v2**) and hybrid retrieval (**Lume**), these outliers expose fundamental engineering tensions:
1. **Entity Substantialism vs. Process Transformations:** Are consciousnesses distinct components, or functional transformation phases of a single event stream? (Kalupahana vs. Xuanzang).
2. **The Ninth Consciousness (*Amalavijñāna*):** Does an agent require an unconditioned, immutable ground truth prior beyond the mutable storehouse? (Paramārtha vs. Faxiang).
3. **Individual vs. Universal Consciousness:** Does multi-agent interaction permit a shared mutable storehouse, or does causal isolation demand strictly individual minds? (Vasubandhu vs. McEvilley/Huayan).
4. **The Trap of the Subliminal Storehouse:** Why did the highest contemplative traditions (Dzogchen) and the most rigorous logicians (Dignāga & Dharmakīrti) actively warn against or completely discard the *ālayavijñāna*?
5. **The Container World (*Bhājana-loka*):** How does the storehouse maintain continuous subliminal spatial and environmental grounding?
6. **Mutual Perfuming (*Tathatā-vāsanā*):** How homeostatic energy gradients actively resist entropy and hallucination drift (Wonhyo).

## 3. Detailed Outlier Analyses & Cognitive Architecture Translations

### 3.1 Outlier 1: Kalupahana's Anti-Substantialist Critique (*Pariṇāma-traya*)

#### Doctrinal Finding
In *A History of Buddhist Philosophy: Continuities and Discontinuities* (1992, pp. 137–143), David Kalupahana argues that interpreting the eight consciousnesses as eight distinct substantial categories (*dravya*) is a foundational misinterpretation perpetuated by later commentators (e.g., Sthiramati, Xuanzang):
* Vasubandhu’s foundational text (*Triṃśikā-vijñaptimātratā*, Verses 1–2) does not define eight separate ontological structures. Rather, it introduces **three transformations of consciousness** (*vijñāna-pariṇāma*):
  1. **Resultant / Maturation (*Vipāka*):** The continuous, unbroken stream of life-process (*citta-santāna*), flowing without reflective self-awareness. It is the raw causal continuum containing all memory seeds (*bīja*).
  2. **Mentation / Valuation (*Manana*):** Self-consciousness, ego-interest, and cognitive bias (*ātma-dṛṣṭi*, *ātma-moha*, *ātma-māna*, *ātma-sneha*). It defiles the stream by imposing self-referential valuation.
  3. **Object-Representation (*Viṣayavijñapti*):** The conceptualization of discrete external objects. Vasubandhu explicitly critiques this third transformation because it causes the agent to reify cognitive representations (*vijñapti*) into permanent, self-existent external entities (*sad-artha*).

#### Engine Mapping (Ferricula v2 Two-Plane Architecture)
* **Reification Hazard in Agent Design:** Modern memory engineers often attempt to build "an episodic agent", "a semantic agent", "a sensory agent", and "a storehouse agent" as isolated microservices with complex IPC. Kalupahana’s reading proves this is an ontological error.
* **The Single-Stream Pipeline:** In Ferricula v2, memory is an unbroken append-only event stream (the `OverlayLog` and WAL). The three "consciousnesses" are merely stages of transaction processing:
  * Stage 1 (*Vipāka*): Ingestion and deterministic event append (`RecordObservation`).
  * Stage 2 (*Manana*): Gating, candidate valuation, and status transition (`Candidate` vs. `Supported`).
  * Stage 3 (*Viṣayavijñapti*): Lexical projection, query rendering, and downstream text synthesis (`EpisodeBundle`).
* The projection is an ephemeral view (*vijñapti*), while the causal log (*santāna*) is the durable ground truth.

### 3.2 Outlier 2: The Ninth Consciousness (*Amalavijñāna*) — Paramārtha & Tiantai

#### Doctrinal Finding
* Indian translator **Paramārtha** (499–569 CE) and the Chinese **Shelun school** (*Shèdàshènglùn-zōng*) posited a **Ninth Consciousness**: the *Amalavijñāna* (Sanskrit: *amala-vijñāna*; Chinese: 無垢識, *wúgòushí*, "immaculate" or "pure" consciousness).
* **The Theological Dilemma:** If the 8th consciousness (*ālayavijñāna*) is conditioned, impermanent, and accumulated through contaminated karmic impressions (*vāsanās*), how can genuine, uncorrupted liberation ever arise from it? Paramārtha posited the 9th consciousness as an innately pure, unconditioned bedrock beneath the *ālaya*, bridging Yogācāra with Tathāgatagarbha (inherent Buddha-nature).
* **The Xuanzang Rejection:** Xuanzang and the orthodox Faxiang school vehemently rejected the 9th consciousness. They argued that positing an unconditioned 9th consciousness violated the core Buddhist doctrine of non-self (*anātman*), smuggling in a Hindu *ātman*. They insisted that the 8th consciousness itself is purified through *āśraya-parāvṛtti* (revolution of the basis), transforming into the Great Mirror Wisdom (*ādarśa-jñāna*), requiring no extraneous 9th layer.

#### Engine Mapping (The Immutable Base vs. Writable Overlay)
* Paramārtha's 9th vs. Xuanzang's 8th debate precisely mirrors the fundamental design question of **agent base knowledge vs. learned episodic overlays**:
  * **The 9th Consciousness (*Amalavijñāna*):** The immutable, read-only recovery base volume (`/data/steve-memory`, mounted strictly `:ro`, containing 3,362 memories and 556 keystones). It is structurally protected from corruption, providing unshakeable priors that cannot be mutated by online experience.
  * **The 8th Consciousness (*Ālayavijñāna*):** The writable state overlay volume (`memory-overlay.json`). It accumulates transient observations, candidate hypotheses, and decay ticks.
  * **Resolution:** Rather than choosing between Paramārtha and Xuanzang, Ferricula implements both: the `:ro` volume acts as *Amalavijñāna* (incorruptible foundation), while the overlay log undergoes continuous *āśraya-parāvṛtti* (consolidation and pruning) under the single-writer commit boundary.

### 3.3 Outlier 3: Universal vs. Individual Ālaya (McEvilley & Huayan Metaphysics)

#### Doctrinal Finding
* As noted by Thomas McEvilley (cited in Wikipedia), classical Yogācāra (Vasubandhu, Asaṅga) held that each individual sentient being possesses their **own separate, private *ālayavijñāna*** (*pratipurūṣa-saṃtāna*).
* However, in Chinese **Faxiang** and **Huayan** metaphysics, this multiplicity was discarded in favor of a single universal, eternal *ālayavijñāna*—a "primordial unity" analogous to Plotinus's Neoplatonic World Soul / Universal Mind.
* **Why Orthodox Indian Yogācāra Rejected Universal Ālaya:**
  1. *Karmic Cross-Contamination:* If the storehouse were universal, Person A committing an error would perfume Person B's mindstream.
  2. *Simultaneous Liberation:* If one being achieved enlightenment, the universal storehouse would be purified, instantaneously liberating all beings.
  3. *Perceptual Duality:* Individual perspectives (*ātmabhāva*) would collapse into undifferentiated monism.

#### Engine Mapping (Multi-Agent Swarm Memory & The "Empty Throne")
* **The Multi-Agent Shared Memory Fallacy:** In modern LLM swarm engineering, a common failure mode is deploying a single shared vector database (e.g., a shared Qdrant/Chroma instance) where all subagents read and write unconstrained embeddings.
* **The Classical Warning:** A shared mutable vector store creates catastrophic **karmic cross-contamination**: Agent A's speculative hallucination enters the shared space, gets retrieved by Agent B as ground truth, and reinforces an unverified loop across the swarm.
* **Ferricula's Invariant:** Respects the orthodox Indian requirement: **One Situated Agent = One Private Memory Stream**. In our n8 swarm:
  * Each agent (Goldfish, Armadillo, Whippet, Stork) owns its private, isolated state volume.
  * Knowledge transfer occurs strictly through explicit, attributed, and typed message passing (*pravṛtti-vijñāna*), never through shared implicit vector contamination.

### 3.4 Outlier 4: The Dzogchen Outlier — Kunzhi (Neutral Ground) vs. Rigpa (Awareness)

#### Doctrinal Finding
In the Tibetan **Dzogchen** (Atiyoga) tradition (Longchenpa, Jigme Lingpa), the 8th consciousness (*kunzhi namshe*, ཀུན་གཞི་རྣམ་ཤེས) is subjected to a radical deconstruction:
* **The Storehouse as a Trap:** The *kunzhi namshe* is categorized as an ordinary, samsaric, conditioned structure—a passive receptacle of habitual latencies (*bag-chags*).
* **The Hazard of Inattentive Quiescence:** If a practitioner quiets the active senses and rests in the passive stillness of the *kunzhi*, they fall into a neutral, un-cognizing, blank stupor (*lung-ma-bstan*). It feels peaceful, but it is merely the idle state of the database, devoid of discriminating intelligence.
* **Rigpa (Pristine Awareness):** True liberation requires transcending the *kunzhi* into *Rigpa*—the non-dual, self-cognizing, active intelligence that directly recognizes the empty, luminous nature of the ground.

#### Engine Mapping (The Vector Store Idle Trap & DenseAM Physics)
* **The Passive Vector Store Fallacy:** An AI memory system that relies solely on embedding retrieval ($k$-NN over an idle vector bank) is trapped in the *kunzhi* stupor:
  * It can match semantic proximity, but it possesses zero meta-evaluative awareness of truth, provenance, or logical consistency.
  * In statistical mechanics (arXiv:2601.01253, Rooke et al.), this corresponds to the **$k > 2$ finite-temperature spurious null-state failure mode**: the network gets trapped in unaligned, high-entropy shallow basins that fail to retrieve genuine signal.
* **The Need for Executive Meta-Cognition (*Rigpa*):** An autonomous system cannot simply be a passive vector store. It requires an active, reflexive verification engine (the 7th gate / hypothesis verifier) that audits candidate retrievals against falsifiable real-world evidence (*arthakriyā*).

### 3.5 Outlier 5: The Dignāga-Dharmakīrti Epistemic Abolition of the Ālaya

#### Doctrinal Finding
As documented by Dan Lusthaus (*Buddhist Phenomenology*, 2002), the greatest logicians of the Buddhist epistemic tradition—**Dignāga** (c. 480–540 CE) and **Dharmakīrti** (c. 6th–7th century CE)—**completely eliminated the *ālayavijñāna* from their formal epistemology**:
* **The Philosophical Reason:** Dignāga and Dharmakīrti realized that an unobservable, metaphysical storehouse holding unmanifest "seeds" was indistinguishable from the Hindu Sāṅkhya concept of *prakṛti* (primal unmanifest matter) or the Vedāntic *ātman* (transcendental Self). It could not be verified by either of the two valid instruments of knowledge (*pramāṇa*): direct perception (*pratyakṣa*) or valid inference (*anumāna*).
* **The Elegant Replacement:** Dignāga and Dharmakīrti demonstrated that cognitive continuity and memory retrieval do not require an ontological storehouse. They explained the entire mind using only:
  1. **Immediate Causal Succession (*Samanantara-pratyaya*):** Each discrete moment of consciousness (*citta-kṣaṇa*) directly conditions the immediate next moment in an append-only causal sequence (*citta-santāna*).
  2. **Reflexive Awareness (*Svasaṃvedana*):** Every cognitive event cognizes itself simultaneously with its object, creating an intrinsic self-verifying trace without requiring a second observer.
  3. **Apoha Theory (Negative Boundary Exclusion):** Conceptual thought (*vikalpa*) operates not by grasping positive essences, but by contrastive boundary exclusion: $A \equiv \neg(\neg A)$.
  4. **Causal Efficacy (*Arthakriyā-sāmarthya*):** The sole criterion of reality is downstream practical utility.

* **Lume as the Dharmakīrtian Engine:** While Ferricula models the living thermodynamic *ālaya*, **Lume** embodies the Dignāga-Dharmakīrti logico-epistemic framework:

### 3.7 Outlier 7: Wonhyo's "Mutual Perfuming" (*Tathatā-Vāsanā*)

#### Doctrinal Finding
* In orthodox Yogācāra, *vāsanā* (perfuming) is an asymmetric process: active defilements perfume the storehouse, which can only be cleansed through laborious, conscious countermeasures (*kuśala-vāsanā*).
* In Korean master **Wonhyo**'s (617–686 CE) commentary on the *Treatise on Awakening Mahāyāna Faith* (*Mahāyāna-śraddhotpāda-śāstra*), he formulates the doctrine of **Mutual Perfuming**:
  * Defilement perfumates Suchness (*tathatā*), generating ignorance and conceptual proliferation (*prapañca*).
  * Simultaneously, **Suchness actively perfumates defilement!**
  * Suchness is not passive; it exerts an innate, continuous, inward homeostatic pull on the mind, naturally inspiring disillusionment with error and guiding the cognitive system back toward coherence and truth.

#### Engine Mapping (Thermodynamic Homeostasis & Falsification Attractors)
* **The Problem of Entropy in AI Memory:** Unconstrained memory accumulation inevitably descends into noise, hallucination drift, and semantic decay.
* **The Inward Pull of Ground Truth:** Wonhyo’s *Tathatā-vāsanā* is the exact computational equivalent of an **innate thermodynamic energy minimum**:
  * In Ferricula's Epanechnikov LSR energy landscape, verified invariant facts (keystones) act as deep attractor wells.

### 3.8 Outlier 8: The Faxiang *Icchantika* Outlier (Architectural Lineage Constraints)

#### Doctrinal Finding
* Xuanzang's Faxiang school maintained the controversial **Five Lineages Doctrine** (*pañcagotra*).
* In contrast to mainstream Mahāyāna (which asserted universal Buddha-nature), Faxiang insisted that a category of beings called **Icchantikas** (一闡提) completely lacked untainted seeds (*anāsrava-bīja*) in their *ālayavijñāna*.
* Because they possessed only conditioned, samsaric seeds, an Icchantika could **never, under any circumstances, achieve Buddhahood**. This doctrine asserted hard, immutable architectural boundaries on cognitive evolution.

#### Engine Mapping (Hardware & Inductive Bias Limits)
* While spiritually controversial, this outlier reflects an unavoidable computational reality:
* **The Icchantika Invariant in AI:** An agent lacking specific physical hardware interfaces, sensor modalities, or mathematical kernels cannot synthesize those capabilities purely through software prompts.
  * A pure text LLM lacking an acoustic frontend (the SigLIP audio void) is structurally an "Icchantika" with respect to raw audio: no amount of fine-tuning can give it direct hearing without introducing an acoustic encoder (CLAP).
  * A runtime lacking a GPU kernel or compiler cannot fabricate compute; it must explicitly acknowledge its structural boundary.
