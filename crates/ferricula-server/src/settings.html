JEV gate backup (TypeSafe)

Gates are fast yes/no and choice judgments made before the language model is asked: whether something is worth researching, and whether to speak or write. The local Ollaya model answers them. JEV, a hosted judge, can take over when Ollaya can't judge (sidecar down, or the text is too long for its 512-token window), or answer first. Answers stay advisory until calibrated.
